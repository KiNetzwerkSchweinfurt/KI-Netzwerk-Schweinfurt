## Third Meetup - AI Network Schweinfurt

After already two AI Network events, one thing was clear: the demand for exchange around Artificial Intelligence in the region and the desire for networking is high. The first two meetups of the AI Network Schweinfurt were received extremely well and showed how much expertise comes together here. That is exactly what this is about – learning from each other, building connections, and growing together. Building on this, the network now enters its next round – this time with input from **Eva Lindenberg** (business mentor) and **Jacob Munke** (Jarm.ai).

## Why an AI Network in Schweinfurt?

- **Strong industrial base:** Schweinfurt has experienced domain experts and many concrete application areas for AI.
- **Missing exchange space:** A regular, open format for practical AI exchange is still lacking.
- **Goal of the network:** Share experiences, lower barriers, and learn from one another.
- **Practical focus:** Real use cases, best practices, tools, and discussions.
- **Knowledge transfer:** Connecting industry, academia, and the developer community.

## Target Group

- Software developers, engineers, and technical teams
- Industry and domain experts
- Students, researchers, and educators
- Practitioners who want to better understand AI workflows and apply them in practice
- SMEs, start-ups, and decision-makers in the region
- People interested in AI and AI enthusiasts

## Output / Outcome

- Understanding of what sets an AI agent apart from just another tool – and when using one in a team really pays off
- Concrete approaches to how agentic workflows take over routine work instead of creating additional complexity
- Insight into embodied AI: how an AI model becomes an acting machine, and the role vision models and sensors play in that
- A realistic assessment of where humanoid robotics stands today – and what that means for production and industry in the region in the medium term
- New contacts in the Schweinfurt industrial landscape, across industries and with potential for cooperation
