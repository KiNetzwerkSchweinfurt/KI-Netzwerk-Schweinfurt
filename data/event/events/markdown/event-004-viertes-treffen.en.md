## Fourth Meetup - AI Network Schweinfurt

After three successful AI Network events, one thing was clear: the demand for exchange around Artificial Intelligence in the region and the desire for networking remains high. The previous meetups of the AI Network Schweinfurt were received extremely well and showed how much expertise comes together here. That is exactly what this is about – learning from each other, building connections, and growing together. Building on this, the network now enters its fourth round.

## Planned Schedule

- 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM: Official part of the event with talks and small-group work
- 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM: Open exchange and networking between participants and experts

## Agenda (Draft)

**1. Welcome and introduction round (5 minutes)**

**3. Keynote talk by DIG-IT KMU Bayern + demo (40 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A)**

- Talk: 40 minutes
- Questions & discussion: 10 minutes

The keynote talk is held by [DIG-IT KMU Bayern](https://digit.kmu.bayern/). A real-world use case from everyday work will be automated using the n8n framework. Since mid last year, the chatbot-assisted work paradigm has increasingly been overtaken by agentic, autonomous automation workflows. The challenge lies in automating a use case end-to-end and safely integrating it into existing company systems in compliance with company policy in order to implement one's own agent workflows.

This session features a live demonstrator showing step by step how a use case is implemented in n8n. Powerful local models are connected to n8n via Ollama for LLM usage.

## Why an AI Network in Schweinfurt?

- **Strong industrial base:** Schweinfurt has experienced domain experts and many concrete application areas for AI.
- **Missing exchange space:** A regular, open format for practical AI exchange is still lacking.
- **Goal of the network:** Share experiences, lower barriers, and learn from one another.
- **Practical focus:** Real use cases, best practices, tools, and discussions.
- **Knowledge transfer:** Connecting industry, academia, and the developer community.

## Target Group

- Software developers, engineers, and technical teams
- Industry and domain experts
- Students, researchers, and educators
- Practitioners who want to better understand AI workflows and apply them in practice
- SMEs, start-ups, and decision-makers in the region
- People interested in AI and AI enthusiasts

## Output / Outcome

- Strong networking with experts from industry, SMEs, and academia
- Know-how building through collaborative learning - learning is social
- Practical impulses for AI in development, engineering, and production
- Exchange on tools, standards, experiences, and real-world challenges
- Strengthening the visibility and future viability of Schweinfurt as a location
