## subtitle

The AI Network Schweinfurt connects people who want to apply artificial intelligence in everyday work, business, and public service, not just discuss it.

## intro

We are an open initiative from the Mainfranken region. Our focus is on trusted exchange, honest lessons learned, and concrete next steps - from the first idea to a pilot project.

## Code of Conduct
### How our meetups work

The AI Network Schweinfurt is an open and free format for everyone interested in artificial intelligence – regardless of prior knowledge, role, or organization.

The focus is on AI technologies, concepts, methods, tools, frameworks, and practical experience. The meetups are intended for professional exchange and shared learning, not for self-promotion, sales pitches, or advertising personal services.

We treat each other with respect, listen actively, let others finish speaking, and avoid confrontational discussions. Different perspectives are welcome.

The goal is not to decide what is right or wrong, but to understand different viewpoints, learn from each other, and broaden our perspective.

## participants-lead

The network thrives on diversity. We welcome everyone who wants to explore AI thoughtfully and responsibly - across industries, company sizes, and professional backgrounds.

## participants-closing

Whether you are planning first steps or already scaling projects, you will find peers, sparring partners, and often the one tip that moves you forward. Public formats are usually free or clearly communicated.
